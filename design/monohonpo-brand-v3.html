A B O U T · T H E S H O P

One workbench.
One honest pair of eyes.

"I started MonoHonpo in 2019 after fifteen years repairing instruments for a Tokyo test-equipment importer. The shop exists so that serious tools leave Japan with their story intact — tested, cleaned, and signed."

Every listing is handled by one person. Every item sits on the bench for a full session before it is photographed, graded, and packed. If an item doesn't test clean, it doesn't list.

We specialize in industrial sensors & test gear, vintage Japanese audio, and hand-selected specialist tools. US buyers get DDP on every order — no surprise fees at delivery.

10 · Gadget & Electronics Mode — wa-modern system applied to tech products Precision · Measured · Warm neutral
T E C H · A P P L I E D

Gadgets on washi, not against it.

The same editorial grammar, tuned for precision instruments, cameras, and consumer electronics — where measurements, ports, and firmware matter more than patina.

Rule 01
Measured photography
Grid-aligned product shots on paper backdrop. Ruler or coin for scale — never decorative props.
Rule 02
Spec block, visible
Every gadget card carries a condensed spec strip — ports, voltage, firmware — above the price.
Rule 03
Dark precision panel
Sumi-ink card variant for flagship electronics — reads as instrument-panel, retains shu seal.
Rule 04
Voltage & region note
All electronics declare 100V-origin and US compatibility inline. No ambiguity at checkout.
