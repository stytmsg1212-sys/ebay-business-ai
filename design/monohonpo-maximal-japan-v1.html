RULE 01 · DO
Pattern as frame, not background
A 4–8px wagara strip across the top or side of a card. Product imagery stays on plain paper.
RULE 02 · DO
Indigo for structural moments
Section headers, footers, sub-brand lockups. Shu remains the seal-of-quality accent.
RULE 03 · DON'T
No wagara behind product
Gadgets, lenses, and electronics photograph on the plain paper or sumi backdrops only.
